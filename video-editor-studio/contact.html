Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround time varies based on project complexity. Short-form videos typically take 3-5 business days, while longer projects like wedding films can take 2-3 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

How do I send my footage?

We accept footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or any cloud storage platform. We'll provide detailed instructions and a secure upload link after you confirm your project.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! All packages include 2 rounds of revisions. Additional revisions can be purchased if needed. We work closely with you to ensure the final product meets your vision.

What video formats do you work with?

We work with all major video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and professional formats like ProRes and RAW footage from various cameras.

Can you add music and sound effects?

Absolutely! We have access to extensive royalty-free music and sound effects libraries. You can also provide your own music, and we'll ensure proper licensing is handled.

What are your payment terms?

We require 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery of the final video. We accept all major payment methods including credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.
