Our Departments & Services

Comprehensive medical care across multiple specialties

Cardiology

Our Cardiology department provides comprehensive heart care services including diagnostic testing, interventional procedures, and cardiac rehabilitation. We use the latest technology and treatment protocols to ensure the best outcomes for our patients.

  • Echocardiography
  • Cardiac Catheterization
  • Angioplasty & Stenting
  • Pacemaker Implantation
Book Consultation

Neurology

Our Neurology department specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the nervous system, including the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. We offer advanced neurological care with a multidisciplinary approach.

  • Stroke Management
  • Epilepsy Treatment
  • Movement Disorders
  • Neuromuscular Diseases
Book Consultation

Orthopedics

Our Orthopedics department provides expert care for musculoskeletal conditions. From sports injuries to joint replacements, our team of orthopedic surgeons and specialists delivers personalized treatment plans.

  • Joint Replacement Surgery
  • Sports Medicine
  • Arthroscopy
  • Fracture Care
Book Consultation

General Medicine

Our General Medicine department provides comprehensive primary care services. Our physicians are trained to diagnose and treat a wide range of medical conditions, serving as your first point of contact for healthcare needs.

  • Routine Health Checkups
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Preventive Care
  • Health Counseling
